# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

First release of ITAM: open-source IT asset management derived from the asset side of the AIM platform.

- Asset register, import from Excel and CSV, QR labels, custom fields, files and comments.
- Custody receipts signed on screen or by link, loaner bookings, software licences, accessories and consumables.
- Physical audits by QR scan, retiring assets, work orders with due times and fault reports from QR labels.
- Employee portal, reports, users and roles, Microsoft single sign-on, two-factor authentication, read-only REST API.
- Installation wizard with language and currency, Arabic and English throughout.
